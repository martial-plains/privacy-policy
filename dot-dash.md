This Privacy Policy outlines how Allister Isaiah Harvey ("Service Provider") manages the information collected through the Dot Dash app ("Application"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled responsibly.

### **Information We Collect and How We Use It**

The Dot Dash app does not collect any personal information upon downloading or using the Application. Registration is not required. The Application uses local storage solely to save user preferences, such as Morse code symbol settings. This data is stored on your device and is used exclusively to enhance the functionality of the Application.

#### **Android App**

In addition to the above, the Android version of the Dot Dash app utilizes the Firebase Crashlytics library for crash reporting and performance diagnostics. However, **Firebase Crashlytics is disabled by default**. Users can choose to enable crash reporting at any time via the app's settings.

* **If enabled**, Firebase Crashlytics collects non-personal diagnostic information, including device details (such as model, OS version), crash logs, and app performance metrics. This data is used solely to improve app performance and stability and is not linked to any personally identifiable data.

* **If disabled**, no data is collected or transmitted to Firebase.

You can manage this setting at any time in the app's settings.

### **Location Information**

The Dot Dash app does not collect precise real-time location information from your device.

### **Sharing of Information**

Since the Application does not collect or transmit personal data by default, no information is shared with third parties. All user preferences are stored locally on your device and are not sent to external servers. The information collected by Firebase Crashlytics (if enabled) is used solely for app performance improvement and is not associated with personal data or shared with third parties.

### **Opt-Out Rights**

You can stop all data collection by **uninstalling the Application**. Additionally, you can manage local storage settings through your device’s settings. If you have enabled Firebase Crashlytics, you can also disable crash reporting at any time via the app's settings. Uninstalling the app will delete any locally stored preferences, and crash reporting will stop if it was previously enabled.

### **Children’s Privacy**

The Dot Dash app is designed with privacy in mind and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are under 13, please do not use the Application or provide any personal information. Parents and guardians should supervise their children’s use of mobile applications and encourage them to avoid entering personal information. If you believe that a child under 13 has inadvertently provided personal information through the Application, please contact us at [a.i.harvey@icloud.com](mailto:a.i.harvey@icloud.com) so we can address the situation.

### **Security**

While the Application does not collect or transmit personal data, we are committed to safeguarding the confidentiality of any user preferences stored locally on your device. We recommend that you regularly review your device’s settings to manage and secure your local data.

If Firebase Crashlytics is enabled, the collected crash data is securely transmitted to Firebase servers over encrypted channels to ensure data security.
